You can read music. What is slow is starting from do and counting up every single time, and counting never turns into reading. You learn the counting path first, then watch the hints come away one step at a time, until a position you used to count is one you read.

- Treble and bass switch on and off separately, and each one gets its own ledger-line reach. The range you picked is drawn on a staff before you start.
- Five stages, from reading the scale to answering with no hints. The first two show the whole counting path before asking one note.
- A session is 10 to 50 questions, or runs until you stop. A wrong answer never spells the right one out.
- The Stats tab colours a staff map note by note so a weak register shows in the shape you read, and Weak spots asks those notes more often.
- The Records tab leaves one row per session, and any session can be played again with the same settings.
- Records are kept per name, so several people on one device do not end up in one history.

Records and accounts stay on the device. No sign-in needed.
