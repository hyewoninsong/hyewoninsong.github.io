A plan without an hour on it is only an intention. Drag an empty stretch of the day's timeline and that stretch becomes the time for it — and when the day goes differently, you drag the block.

- Placing a todo without typing a start or an end — the drag is the time
- An all-day list for things that only need doing, not scheduling
- Moves that never ask you a question, and fifty steps of undo behind them
- Link once, drag once, and everything after it follows
- A drawer that holds what you skipped instead of deleting it — it comes back with its spacing intact
- A record that counts only what you checked off — a contribution grid, streaks, per-period hours
- A profile per person, and each device remembers its own

Your plan lives on your device and in your own iCloud. There is no account to create and no sign-in screen.
