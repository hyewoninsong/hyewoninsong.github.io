Writing down what to do and deciding when to do it are two different jobs, and a list only ever does the first. Here you drag an empty stretch of the day's timeline and that stretch becomes the time for it — and when the day goes differently, you drag the block.

- Placing a task without typing a start or an end — the drag is the time
- Moves that never ask you a question, and fifty steps of undo behind them
- Link once, drag once, and everything after it follows
- A drawer that holds what you skipped instead of deleting it — it comes back with its spacing intact
- History that accumulates without being written down — streaks, per-period hours, a contribution grid
- Separate profiles for work and study, and each device remembers its own

Your plans and history move only inside your own iCloud account. There is no account to create and no sign-in screen.
